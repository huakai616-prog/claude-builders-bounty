# Section briefs (who plays what)

Bars are the score's bar numbers. "RH"/"LH" = the piano's right / left hand
in `piano_source.json`. Each brief fixes the texture; the drafter chooses
octaves, bowings and details within ARRANGING.md.

Note on `--check` BASS: every left-hand attack counts as "bass". Where the
LH is a broken-chord / 16th figure, the player of that figure must be the
lowest part ATTACKING at those moments (a cello note held by a tie doesn't
count as an attack) — or the cello must attack the same pitch class.

Common to all sections:
- Set a dynamic for every part that plays, on its first note in the section.
- Don't tie across the section's last barline; end cleanly.
- If you change a clef (viola treble, cello tenor) or start an 8va line,
  undo it inside your section.

## S01 · bars 0–15 · Intro 前奏 (music box, then chorale)
- 0–4 (pp): two violins alone, *sul tasto*. Vn I = RH melody an octave
  below the piano's sounding 8va (so the written notes of the source), legato
  slurs by phrase. Vn II = LH sustained notes (G5 dotted half + B♭5 quarter
  etc.) at pitch. Va, Vc tacet.
- 5–15 (p): chorale. Vn I = top note of the RH chords (again an octave below
  sounding), Vn II = the RH inner note(s) (single line, or double stop on the
  tied longer chords), Va = the LH broken-chord 8ths (one player, legato in
  2-bar slurs or per half bar), Vc = bass: the lowest LH note of each
  harmony as a sustained note an octave down (E♭2, G2, F2 …), long bows.
  In 9–15 the RH has a second voice (counter-line in 8ths): give it to Vn II
  and move Vn II's chord notes into Va double stops only where playable,
  otherwise drop the least important chord tone (keep the melody, the
  counter-line and the bass). Build a small crescendo into 15.

## S02 · bars 16–23 · Riff 主题动机
- 16 (subito f) and 18 (f): tutti rhythmic hits in the piano's 3+3+2 rhythm,
  marcato, down-bows; chords spread over the four parts (double stops in the
  violins/viola where playable); Vc = the low G 16th figure of the LH.
- 17 (mp): Vn I = the RH high 16th figure (tenuto top notes, string
  crossings, slur per beat); Va = the LH 16th figure (B♭4 G4 A4 …) at pitch
  or 8vb; Vn II = sustained chord tone; Vc = G2 held.
- 19–22 (ff): the riff. Vn I = RH top line (the high 16ths), Vn II = the
  RH line an octave lower (the RH plays octaves) or the chord tones of the
  RH chords; Va = LH chord stabs and the mid-register 16ths; Vc = LH bass
  (G2 / E♭2 …) with the LH's low 16th rhythm. Accents as in the piano.
- 23: decrescendo transition, thin out toward the verse; ends p.

## S03 · bars 24–31 · Verse 主歌 (p)
- Vn I = RH melody (top voice), cantabile, phrase slurs.
- Vn II and Va = the RH inner chord tones, sustained / in the piano's rhythm
  but smoothed (tie repeated inner notes), one note each (double stops only
  if a chord would otherwise be incomplete).
- Vc = LH: the lower note of each LH dyad (whole notes) — take the upper LH
  note into Va when Va is free. Warm, long bows.

## S04 · bars 32–35 · Pre-Chorus 导歌 (build)
- Va = the LH 16th ostinato (G3 F3 G3 D3 …) exactly, détaché.
- Vc = the LH's low quarter-note bass (G2 …).
- Vn I = RH upper voice (syncopated A4 B♭4 A4 G4 line); Vn II = RH lower
  voice (F4 D4 D4 …).
- 34–35: crescendo; the chords of 34–35 spread over all four; the triplet
  in 35 in Va or Vc. Land ff on 36.

## S05 · bars 36–49 · Chorus 副歌 (ff)
- Vn I = RH melody (top notes), full sound; octave double stops only on the
  long notes where playable.
- Vn II = RH chord tones below the melody (double stops in the dotted
  rhythm of the RH chords, marcato).
- Va = LH middle layer: the 8th/16th chord stabs and then the descending
  16th arpeggios (transposed into viola range), detached, accented as the
  piano.
- Vc = LH bass: the low whole notes / octaves (take them at G2, C2, D2 …)
  plus the LH's low 8ths; in 44–47 the LH's accented low 8ths → Vc marcato.
- 48–49: the piano's upward/downward 16th sweeps → Va/Vc; 49 closes the
  chorus (held chord).

## S06 · bars 50–57 · Interlude 间奏 (mp → f)
- Vn II = the RH 16th broken pattern (D5 G5 D5 …), light spiccato, one
  player.
- Vn I = the tenuto top notes of the RH (melody) from 53 on; before that
  (50–52) Vn I doubles the long RH chord tones or rests.
- Va = LH chord tones (half notes), Vc = LH bass whole notes and the
  dotted-quarter/8th rhythm.
- 57: LH run up (clef change in the piano) → Va/Vc hand-off.

## S07 · bars 58–65 · Bridge 桥段 → climax
- 58–61: Vn I = RH melody (octaves in the piano: take the upper octave
  if ≤ C7 else lower), Vn II = the RH lower octave; Va = LH 16th figure
  (treble-clef LH, bring it into viola range, treble clef OK); Vc = LH
  lowest notes, sustained.
- 62–65: climax chords 8va: Vn I/Vn II double stops on the RH chords
  (upper notes), Va = LH 16th pattern, Vc = LH low notes; 65: the big
  accented chords (tutti, rolled triple stops, marcato), ff.

## S08 · bars 66–73 · Solo 华彩 (the runs)
- The quintuplet/sextuplet runs are played as a RELAY: the run starts in Va
  (low part), continues in Vn II (middle), and Vn I takes the top and the
  descending quintuplets of the next bar. Overlap by one note at each hand-
  off so the line is seamless; keep the tuplet grouping of the piano.
- The RH's held chord at the start of 66/68/70/72 (the whole-note chord) =
  struck by Vn I + Vn II (double stops) on beat 1 and held until they enter
  the relay; Vc = the LH: low octave (at G2/E♭2 …) and the dotted-8th
  rhythm with the chord stab on beat 3 (Va joins the stab when free).

## S09 · bars 74–77 · Arpeggios 琶音 (melody over 16ths)
- Vn I = the accented dotted-8th melody on top of the RH (D6. D6. …).
- Vn II = the RH 16th arpeggio figure below the melody.
- Vc = the LH ascending 16th arpeggio (E♭2 B♭2 F3 G3 …) — cello-friendly;
  Va = sustained chord tones from the LH top notes (half notes).

## S10 · bars 78–81 · Breakdown 崩落
- Beats 1–2: the piano's two-note tremolo ([B♭5 D6] ↔ B♭6, half note) →
  Vn I bowed tremolo `trem3` on the top note (B♭6; in 81 C7), Vn II bowed
  tremolo on the lower dyad as a double stop (B♭5+D6; in 81 C6+F6), Va a
  tremolo chord tone below (e.g. B♭4 or D5) for body.
- Beats 3–4: the 32nd cascade is an exact four-player RELAY, one 8th each:
  Vn I plays the first four 32nds (B♭6 E♭6 D6 B♭5), Vn II the next four
  (B♭5 E♭5 D5 B♭4), Va the next four (B♭4 E♭4 D4 B♭3), Vc the last four
  (B♭3 E♭3 D3 B♭2 — the piano's LH does these). Same shape in 79–81 with
  their pitches.
- Vc = the LH: bass on beat 1 (E♭2 / F2 / G2 / D2) with the octave 8ths
  (octave double stops E♭2+E♭3 etc. are fine on the cello), and the LH
  half note on beat 3, then its cascade group.

## S11 · bars 82–89 · Pre-Chorus 导歌 II (like 32–35)
- 82–85: same texture as S04: Va = LH 16th ostinato, Vc = the LH's low
  quarter-note bass (G2 E♭2 F2 G2), Vn I = RH upper voice (A4 B♭4 A4 …),
  Vn II = RH lower voice (F4 D4 D4 …).
- 86–88: four layers: RH 16th figure (D5 A4 B♭4 F4 …) → Vn I; the LH top
  syncopated line (D4 D4 … C4 D4) → Vn II; LH ostinato (G3 B♭3 B♭3 …) → Va;
  LH bass (G2 E♭2 F2) → Vc. Grow (cresc.) through 88.
- 89: the transition: the piano's held chord and the rising triplet
  (G4 A4 B♭4 D5 added one by one) → Vn II/Va/Vc add their notes one by one
  and hold (a string "bloom"); Vn I = the RH's 8va notes (E♭6 D6 D7 C7 …,
  octave down if needed). Arrive p at 90.

## S12 · bars 90–96 · Music Box 八音盒 (p, 8va)
- Vn I = the RH top-note melody (dotted 8ths etc.), an octave below the
  piano's 8va if above C7.
- Vn II = the RH 16th figure under it (one player, light).
- Va = LH middle chord tones (8ths / half notes), Vc = LH lowest notes
  (whole notes), soft.

## S13 · bars 97–107 · Chorus 副歌 II + Interlude
- 97–103: Vn I = RH melody top, Vn II = RH chord tones, Va = the LH
  repeated-16th figure (E♭4 E♭4 E♭4 + chords) as driving 16ths (single
  notes, or two-note chord where the LH has one), Vc = LH low notes.
- 104–107: like S06 (Vn II = RH 16ths, Vn I = melody, Va/Vc = LH).

## S14 · bars 108–117 · Final Chorus 终副歌 (ff, fullest)
- Vn I = melody (top), octave double stops on long notes where playable.
- Vn II = RH second voice (the 16th inner line in 108–111) or chord tones.
- Va = LH chord stabs and 16th arpeggios; Vc = LH bass octaves (low).
- 112–117: everybody on the big chords (double/triple stops, marcato,
  down-bows), Vc on the LH low octaves. 117 closes (held chord, fermata-like
  weight but in time).

## S15 · bars 118–132 · Coda 尾声 (p → pp)
- 118–121: Vn II = the RH 8th broken figure (G4 B♭4 D5 …); Vn I rests or
  holds the RH's long top note (B♭5 in 118) softly; Va = the LH syncopated
  dyads (B♭3+D4 …) as a double stop or single notes; Vc = the LH bass
  (E♭2 G2 F2 … whole notes).
- 122–127: the LH becomes broken 8ths (E♭3 B♭3 G4 …) → Va (one player);
  Vc = the lowest note of each LH group sustained (E♭3/E♭2, G2, F2 …);
  Vn I = the RH melody (C6 B♭5 A5, D6 …); Vn II = the RH's lower line /
  8ths (G4 B♭4 D5 …, F4 C5 D5 …).
- 128: the RH alone (A5 A5 A5 B♭5 B♭5) → Vn I alone, the others rest
  (a lone voice — keep it naked).
- 129–130: Vn I = the 8va melody (D7 C7 B♭6; octave below if you prefer
  D6 C6 B♭5) with Vn II on the 16ths of the RH's second voice; Va = the LH
  16th run (D4 G4 A4 … C6, treble clef); Vc = G2 (piano G3 an octave down
  or at pitch G3), held.
- 131–132: the final sonority G3 + B♭5 held (tied, fermata on 132): Vc G2
  (open) and/or G3, Va G3/D4, Vn II B♭4/G4, Vn I B♭5 — all pp → niente,
  fermata on the last bar.
